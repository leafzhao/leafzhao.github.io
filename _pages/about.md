I am an experimental physicist at the University of York working on **laser–plasma interaction** and **inertial confinement fusion (ICF)**.

My research focuses on **laser–plasma instabilities** — in particular stimulated Raman scattering in direct-drive and double-cone ignition conditions — and on developing the **diagnostics** needed to measure them, including angular-resolved and fibre-based scattered-light diagnostics and X-ray phase-contrast imaging of laser-driven shocks. I have carried out experiments at large laser facilities such as the Shenguang-II Upgrade laser and GSI PHELIX.
